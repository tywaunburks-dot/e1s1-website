Meet Our Staff

E1S1 is guided by a team committed to Christ-centered care, integrity, and practical transformation.

Ty’Waun Burks

Founder / CEO

Ty’Waun leads E1S1’s vision and programming—building Christ-centered spaces where people can heal, grow, and step into purpose. He focuses on strategy, partnerships, and outcomes that last beyond the retreat.

Scott Stewart

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Scott oversees operations, planning, and execution to ensure every workshop and retreat runs smoothly. He supports systems, logistics, and the practical details that help participants feel safe and supported.

Joyce Hemphill-Rowe

Secretary

Joyce supports communication, documentation, and organizational coordination. She helps keep E1S1 aligned, organized, and responsive to participants, partners, and stakeholders.

Theopolis Batson

Board Member

Theopolis provides oversight and guidance to strengthen E1S1’s mission and impact. He supports accountability, wise decision-making, and community alignment with E1S1’s values.
