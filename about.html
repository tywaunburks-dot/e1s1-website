About E1S1

Encouragement that leads to action—support that leads to breakthrough.

What we do

E1S1 provides Christ-centered experiences that help people heal and rebuild. We combine spiritual foundation with practical tools, creating a clear path forward.

  • Workshops and guided group sessions
  • Retreat experiences focused on restoration
  • Mentorship and accountability support
  • Resources that reinforce lasting growth

Why we exist

Many people feel stuck—carrying pain, confusion, and heavy cycles. E1S1 exists to help people move into freedom and stability through truth, community, and guidance.

Core focus: healing, healthy boundaries, identity, purpose, and community transformation.

Values

Truth + Grace

We offer compassion without compromising truth.

Growth + Accountability

We build real change through consistent next steps.

Community + Impact

We don’t just heal individuals—we strengthen families and communities.
