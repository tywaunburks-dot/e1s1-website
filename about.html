Our story & foundation

About E1S1

Encourage 1 to Save 1 (E1S1) is a Christ-centered community focused on healing, growth, and practical transformation—helping people move from survival to stability to purpose.

What we believe

We believe transformation is possible through Christ, supported by truth, wisdom, and community. E1S1 creates safe spaces where people can face what hurts, grow in identity, and take practical next steps.

Scripture foundation: Matthew 5:14 — “You are the light of the world.”

Our approach

  • Truth + grace: biblical clarity with compassion
  • Community: encouragement, accountability, and support
  • Practical steps: tools that work in real life
  • Ongoing growth: resources before and after events

Mission

To encourage people toward wholeness through Christ-centered guidance, real community, and practical steps that help them overcome and build a stable future.

Vision

A growing network of individuals, families, and communities walking in freedom—equipped to encourage others and bring light where it’s needed most.
