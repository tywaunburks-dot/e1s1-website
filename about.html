About E1S1

Encouragement that leads to action—support that leads to breakthrough.

Our mission

To help first responders, veterans, underserved communities, and families move from survival to stability to purpose— by navigating depression, PTSD, anxiety, and forgiveness through Christ-centered workshops, retreats, mentorship, and community support.

Who we serve

  • First Responders (Police, Fire, EMS, Dispatch, Corrections)
  • Veterans and Military Families
  • Underserved Individuals and Communities
  • Families rebuilding after difficult seasons
  • Youth and Community Leaders

What we provide

Workshops

Teaching + guided reflection with practical tools for boundaries, identity, and forward momentum.

Retreats

Immersive restoration experiences designed to reset your mind, strengthen your spirit, and clarify next steps.

Resources

Tools like the Hero’s Be Free workbook to keep growth going before and after the experience.
