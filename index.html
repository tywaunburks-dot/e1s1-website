We help people move from survival to stability to purpose.

Encourage 1 to Save 1 (E1S1) supports first responders, veterans, underserved communities, and families as they navigate depression, PTSD, anxiety, and the journey of forgiveness—through Christ-centered workshops, retreats, mentorship, and community support.

What is E1S1?

Encourage 1 to Save 1 is a faith-based movement that builds safe spaces for truth, healing, and practical change. Our workshops and retreats help people process pain, rebuild identity, strengthen boundaries, and walk forward with support.
