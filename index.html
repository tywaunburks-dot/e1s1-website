What is E1S1?

E1S1 (Encourage 1 to Save 1) is a Christ-centered movement focused on helping people rebuild after hard seasons. We combine biblical truth, real community, and practical steps that lead to lasting change—not just a moment.

  • Identity: learning who you are in Christ
  • Healing: processing pain without staying stuck
  • Boundaries: building healthy relationships
  • Purpose: moving forward with clarity and direction

Scripture foundation: Matthew 5:14 — “You are the light of the world.”
